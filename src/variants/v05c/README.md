# Variant 05c: The Business Line

A navy-dominant, more restrained refinement of variant 05's British-heritage
grammar, built for professionals first. Where v05 reads as a Penguin
paperback and an Underground platform in equal parts, 05c leans toward a
London private members' club: deeper navy, far less red, a serif numeral
on every plaque instead of a sans-serif signage digit.

New German copy leads with workplace situations (the meeting, the
presentation, the call with London or New York, the job interview) and with
Kym's 35+ years giving seminars and public speaking in English, before
Nachhilfe and one-to-one conversation follow as secondary offers. Business
English is listed first among the three offers and carries the page's one
red accent; Nachhilfe and Conversation keep navy.

## What changed vs. v05

- **Colour balance.** Header, hero, about and contact are now navy (v05 used
  cream/red for those). Red is cut down to single, deliberate accents: the
  primary CTA, the top rule on the lead offer card, the roundel bar, one
  hairline inside each photo frame. Nothing is a solid red block anymore.
- **New "Diese Momente kennst du" section.** Replaces v05's generic
  "Philosophie" manifesto with four concrete workplace scenes (meeting,
  presentation, international call, job interview), laid out as stops along
  a navy line, literally "stops on the line," the motif the v05 README
  already named, now used for content that actually is a sequence.
- **Reordered, not rewritten, offers.** Business English now leads, followed
  by Englischnachhilfe, then conversation. All bullet content from the brief
  is kept; wording is tightened and regrouped here and there.
- **Numbered markers, used once.** The ring-and-bar roundel now numbers the
  five page sections in Roman numerals (I–V) only, a true sequence, instead
  of being repeated on every offer card. Offer cards get an icon medallion
  instead, tied to what the offer actually is.
- **Refined framing.** The oval "postcard" hero frame is replaced with a
  rectangular cream mat and a single red hairline, closer to a framed
  photograph in a corridor than a souvenir postcard.
- **Tighter type and rhythm.** Fluid `clamp()` sizing for the hero headline,
  a touch more line-height on body serif text, smaller and tighter header on
  mobile (no more two-line brand lockup eating vertical space), more
  deliberate section padding for calmer whitespace on desktop.
- **Contrast fix.** v05's pattern of red label text would turn illegible on
  navy backgrounds here, since so many more sections are now navy. Eyebrow
  labels use a red underline (non-text accent) instead of red fill, and
  switch their text colour to cream on dark sections.

## Headline

**"Englisch, mit dem du im Job überzeugst."**
Subline: "Business English · Englischnachhilfe · Konversation im
Einzelunterricht." Seminars-and-public-speaking credibility is introduced
in the very next line, then substantiated in the Business English offer and
the About section.

## Fonts

- **Libre Baskerville**: headings, body copy, pull-quotes, and now also the
  roundel numeral (serif crest instead of sans-serif signage digit).
- **Inter** (variable): nav, labels, eyebrows, buttons.
