# Variant 08: Trusted Advisor

Clean corporate / professional-services design for English with Kym: a deep-navy
and white layout built around a credibility strip (London-born, 30+ years in
Germany, 35+ years running seminars, 15+ years teaching privately), card-based
offer sections, and crisp line icons, the register of a modern consulting firm
or SaaS landing page rather than a tutoring flyer.

## Fonts

- **DM Sans Variable** (`@fontsource-variable/dm-sans`) for all text, weights
  400–700 used for body copy, labels and headings.

## Colour palette

- `#0a1830` / `#0f2748`: near-black navy and primary navy (header text, contact
  band, stats strip background)
- `#163460`: secondary navy for supporting UI text
- `#2454c9` / `#3568e0`: accent blue (CTAs, icons, links, focus ring)
- `#e7edfb`: pale blue tint (icon chips)
- `#f6f8fb` / `#eef1f6` / `#e1e7f0`: light greys (section backgrounds, card
  borders)
- `#46556b`: muted slate for body copy on white
- White cards throughout, all text pairs checked for AA contrast on their
  background.
